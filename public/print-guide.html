5. Linking Your iPhone Google Gemini App ("JARVIS" Gem) With This Command Center

Google's consumer Gemini iOS app keeps custom Gems sandboxed inside Google's mobile app, so it cannot call a local PC server directly by itself — however, you can link them together in 3 seamless ways (built into this update):

  1. Unify Their Brain, Persona & 1-Tap Launch (🛡 Security & Settings):
    • Open your custom JARVIS Gem in the Google Gemini app (or at https://gemini.google.com), copy its Instructions (and optional Gem link).
    • In JARVIS Command Center, open 🛡 Security & Settings → paste both into 📱 Link Your iPhone Google Gemini "JARVIS" Gem → click Save.
    • Both assistants now follow the exact same persona & rules, and the top ✦ Gemini JARVIS button jumps straight to your Gem!
  2. Create a 1-Tap "Send to JARVIS Hub" iPhone Share-Sheet Bridge (Apple Shortcuts):
    • In JARVIS Command Center, open 🛡 Security & Settings and click 📋 Copy iPhone Shortcut Token (this copies your /api/mobile/bridge URL + X-Jarvis-Agent-Token).
    • On your iPhone, open Apple's built-in Shortcuts app → tap + → name it Send to JARVIS Hub → tap the ⓘ icon at the bottom and turn ON Show in Share Sheet (Receive: Text).
    • Add one action: Get Contents of URL:
      • URL: http://10.0.0.85:7777/api/mobile/bridge (or your Tailscale URL for 5G anywhere)
      • Method: POST
      • Headers: Key X-Jarvis-Agent-Token = (paste your token)
      • Request Body (JSON): Key title = iPhone Gemini JARVIS, Key text = Shortcut Input
    • How to use it: Anytime you brainstorm a chapter, app, or plan inside your Google Gemini iOS app, tap Share on Gemini's reply → tap Send to JARVIS Hub. It instantly appears in your Command Center chat log and saves a Markdown file into workspace\ios-gemini-inbox\ across your PC and MacBooks!
  3. Shared Notion Executive Hub: Both your iPhone and all 4 laptops can view and collaborate inside your live Notion Command Hub: https://app.notion.com/p/41537a4325628115abf7cb2f6474e4e1.
